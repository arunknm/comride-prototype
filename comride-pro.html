GOT QUESTIONS

FAQ

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel anytime from your profile settings. You keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. No fees, no questions asked.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

All your ride history, GPX files, and analytics data are preserved permanently. Pro features simply become inaccessible until you re-subscribe. Nothing is ever deleted.

Is there a free trial?

7 days free on Explorer or Expedition — no credit card required. You'll get a reminder 2 days before the trial ends, and you only pay if you choose to continue.

Can I switch tiers mid-cycle?

Absolutely. Upgrading takes effect immediately and you're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the cycle. Downgrading takes effect at the next renewal.
