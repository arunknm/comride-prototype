New Two-Wheeler Helmet & Speed Rules — Bengaluru City Limits

Effective June 1, 2026, the following rules apply to all two-wheeler riders within BBMP limits:

Mandatory AIS 146 Certified Helmets

Only ISI / AIS 146 marked helmets permitted. Non-certified helmets attract ₹1,000 fine. Pillion rider non-compliance: ₹500.

Revised ORR Speed Limit — 50 kmph

Outer Ring Road (Marathahalli–Hebbal) speed limit for two-wheelers reduced to 50 kmph. 18 new ANPR cameras operational.

Expired Insurance = Impoundment

Riding with expired insurance or invalid PUC certificate will result in vehicle impoundment. Penalty: ₹2,000 + towing charges.
