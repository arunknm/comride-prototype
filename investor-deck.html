The Problem

India has 200M+ riders.
Zero infrastructure built for them.

⚠️

Safety is a Gamble

Riders coordinate on WhatsApp groups with no identity verification. Strangers join rides with zero accountability. India records 4.12 lakh road accidents/year — two-wheelers account for 35%.

📱

No Purpose-Built Platform

Strava is for cyclists. Facebook Groups are unstructured. Existing apps ignore real-time ride coordination, route sharing, or motorcycle-specific communities. The market is completely unserved.

🏍️

Discovery is Broken

Riders spend hours researching routes, hotels, and experiences across scattered platforms. ₹18,000 Cr/year in riding-related spend has no dedicated discovery and booking channel.

The core insight: The 5M+ active motorcycle touring enthusiasts in India represent a high-income, high-engagement demographic with no platform built around their needs — safety, community, discovery, and gear.

Our Moat

Network effects: every verified rider and club that joins makes the platform more valuable for the next. Community data creates an insurmountable lead over any single-feature entrant.

Strava is cycling-focused with western UX. WhatsApp has no ride-specific safety layer. Google Maps has no community. Building all of this from scratch is a 24-month project for any new entrant.

Expansion Defensibility

India-first localisation (Aadhaar KYC, UPI payments, NHAI integration, Hindi/regional language support) is a deep technical moat that western apps cannot replicate quickly.

🛡️

Safety Regulation Push

MoRTH's 2025 road safety mandate requires two-wheeler tracking for commercial operators. Insurance companies mandating digital documentation. Comride is infrastructure for compliance.

🌄

Adventure Travel Surge

Post-COVID domestic adventure tourism up 55%. Ladakh, Spiti, NE India motorcycle routes are Instagram's fastest-growing travel categories in India. Supply side (resorts) actively seeking riders.
